Tour Information

Jeep Safari

Experience an exciting off-road journey through tea plantations, mountain roads, forests, waterfalls and scenic viewpoints. Our jeep safari takes you to places that are difficult to reach by regular vehicles, giving you an unforgettable adventure.

🌄

Sunrise View

Wake up early and witness the spectacular sunrise over the mountains and tea estates. Kolukkumalai is particularly known for its high-altitude sunrise experience, with panoramic views of the surrounding hills and valleys.

🥾

Trekking

Explore Munnar on foot through beautiful mountain trails, tea plantations, forests and natural landscapes. Trekking offers a closer connection with nature and is available across trails with different levels of difficulty.
